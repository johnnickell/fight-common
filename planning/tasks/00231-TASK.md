# Reduce the Functional suite to at most 99 expanded cases

## Outcome

Reduce the entire Functional suite to at most 99 expanded PHPUnit cases without losing distinct behavior checks. The maintainer expanded this TASK from the initial Task Live and Task Cancellation slice to the suite-wide count checkpoint.

## Scope

Continue from the Task Live and Task Cancellation slice and inventory the entire Functional suite. Consolidate repeated full-stack combinations and add focused lower-level checks where existing coverage is insufficient. Keep representative end-to-end and framework smoke cases in Functional, real database and framework boundary checks in Integration, and isolated behavior in Unit. The maintainer cited Fight Access Control's reported 9,419 Unit, 847 Integration, and 7 Functional cases as a reference; roughly 10:100:1,000 Functional:Integration:Unit or better is guidance, not a quota. Do not change production behavior, skip cases to meet the target, or move an unchanged full journey merely to lower the count. Leave release probes, unrelated runner changes and hosted timing to their owning work.

## Use cases and contracts

A maintainer runs PHPUnit and the complete local gate. Representative Functional cases still prove native stream delivery, reconnect behavior and routed cancellation across processes. Real database boundary checks retain locking, transaction and recovery behavior, including supported drivers and material table-layout differences. Preserve meaningful checks for revocation, stream closure, both lock orders, competing terminal outcomes, rollback and lost acknowledgments. The test changes add no product commands, queries, events or runtime permissions.

## Validation and permissions

Use disposable test data and isolated fixtures. Keep the existing suite and coverage gates. Treat a newly confirmed product defect as separate regression-first work rather than changing the expected result to pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Record expanded before/after counts for changed families and the complete Functional suite, with skips reported separately. Reach at most 99 expanded Functional cases and show comparable suite timings with the command, revision and runtime conditions.
- [ ] For each consolidated or moved family, identify which behaviors remain covered, where they run, and any assurance lost by not crossing every combination. Preserve distinct regressions, supported framework smoke behavior and real database checks where the behavior depends on those boundaries.
- [ ] Pass the complete local gate with exact 100% direct Unit coverage. Do not hand off to independent Review before the count and gate both pass.

## Verification and evidence

Use `./bin/phpunit` for focused cases and expanded case enumeration; use comparable fresh Functional runs and `./bin/build` for final verification. Save counts, timing, logs, exits, coverage and the tested revision in the ignored handoff.
