# Clarify MCP capability composition validation

## Outcome

Separate cohesive cross-feature composition validation from generic capability registration/indexing while
retaining the registry as the single trusted construction boundary. Successful construction must mean the same
valid composition as today; consumers must not manage a new validation sequence.

John approved planning this recommendation on 2026-11-02 against develop `521a71c`.
Normally one behavior-preserving PR. Approval of planning does not start implementation or authorize
publication/release.

## Scope

- In scope: `McpCapabilityRegistry`, a cohesive internal composition validator and directly affected registry,
  Skill, Task and subscription composition tests. Cross-feature shared-instance and activation checks move with
  the knowledge they enforce; generic indexing/advertisement responsibilities remain intelligible.
- Preserve public constructor parameters/defaults, lookup methods, server identity, mirror maps, advertised
  definitions, extension merging and subscription access.
- Deliberately classify new internals and reconcile affected CONTEXT/MCP composition descriptions.
- Out of scope: a registry builder, consumer setup steps, new public registration contracts, plugin frameworks,
  dynamic feature discovery, weaker activation rules or claims that extraction removes actual feature coupling.

## Validation and permissions

Keep duplicate method/mirror ownership rejection, standard method/family and mandatory-anchor constraints,
extension conflict semantics, complete Skills/readable Resources composition, exact shared Task/Tool instances,
and truthful subscription activation. Composition is neither authentication nor business authorization. Fresh
availability and disclosure admission remain at their existing request boundaries; do not cache authority or
invoke a provider/Tool to validate registration. No persistence or effect guarantees change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Registry construction and all public lookups retain their accepted signatures and behavior.
- [ ] A cohesive internal owner expresses cross-feature constraints without exposing new consumer sequencing or
  allowing capabilities to self-certify global consistency.
- [ ] Duplicate, contradictory, incomplete and wrongly shared compositions continue to reject.
- [ ] Extension merging retains object/list/type distinctions and applicable registration-order independence.
- [ ] Standard family anchors, reserved methods, mirror ownership and actual subscription/Task activation remain
  truthful; valid custom capabilities remain accepted under the same contract.
- [ ] Registration does not enumerate providers, invoke Tools or introduce runtime effects.
- [ ] Internal classification and affected docs are reconciled; meaningful direct tests and full-gate evidence exist.
