## Reproduction and affected contract

During TASK-00194 integration on develop `3ac1d227622f8978b1fb907aae073b63e1b259e4`, the real commit-hook gate
failed `McpSubscriptionJourneyTest::test_that_independent_routed_operator_queries_summarize_then_page_actual_streams_with_current_inspection_grants@sqlite`
at line 107: expected 2, observed 1. The line contains three assertions; its historical exact cause is not
conclusively proven. The same journey had previously failed with observed 0, interspersed with passing gates.

Bounded diagnosis reproduced a concrete pre-existing test defect: `DbalSubscriberReader` captures its invocation
clock before a live scan; an actual concurrent heartbeat can advance a registration's `observed_at` into the next
second before the row is read. `SubscriberView` correctly reports that future observation as `stale_uncertain`.
The live journey incorrectly requires both ongoing streams to be `recently_observed`, and later similarly assumes
fixed lifecycle state counts. A forced valid clock-boundary interleaving produced two registrations/one subscriber,
both open and heartbeating, but zero recently observed and two stale/uncertain, failing assertion 22. This is not
lease expiry, absent registration or proof that runtime inspection should manufacture connectivity.

The reader, fixture, journey and documented clock behavior predate TASK-00194 and the TASK-00190 moving-base
merge. The exact original expected-2/observed-1 failure remains a historical attribution limit, not a claimed
spontaneous reproduction. Existing private diagnosis and raw evidence are at canonical
`.runs/tasks/TASK-00194/runs/20261104-diagnosis/diagnosis.md`.

[Subscriber inspection](../../docs/subscriber-inspection.md#results-count-units-and-freshness) owns invocation-clock
classification and [live consistency](../../docs/subscriber-inspection.md#live-consistency-and-failures). Future
observations and expired open records remain stale/uncertain; closure evidence and permissions retain their current
meaning. Live reads are not snapshots and do not renew storage.
