# Follow authorized Task state and recover after reconnecting

## Outcome

Authorized clients receive bounded full-state Task notifications through the existing subscription stream, then recover
current state through tasks/get after interruption without treating older responses as newer. John approved this single
TASK as the complete decomposition of [TICKET-00052](../tickets/00052-TICKET.md). Live delivery, ordering and recovery
stay together in one complete PR; a delivery-only slice would leave the required reconnect contract unfinished.

TASK-00162 supplies live transport and technical lifecycle tracking; TASK-00168 supplies complete durable Task operations,
transitively including lifecycle, input and cancellation. These are the only blockers. Subscriber inspection, Prompts,
removal and Mercure are not additional blockers. Ordinary Task operations gain no reverse live dependency.

## Scope

- In scope: optional taskIds subscription integration, truthful negotiation, acknowledgment/correlation, shared current
  Task sourcing/projection, fresh full-state authority and actual live HTTP delivery with existing technical tracking.
- In scope: authorized initial baseline, bounded coalescing, comparable Task-scoped revisions on reads/notifications,
  stale-response protection, reconnect/current-state recovery and privacy-safe unavailable/failure behavior.
- In scope: actual SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL and independent-process/HTTP evidence, affected native stream compatibility,
  schema/API classification, documentation/CONTEXT.md, exact owned-code coverage and full build.
- Out of scope: Task transition history/replay/audit ledger, per-message acknowledgments/receipts, guaranteed delivery,
  mandatory domain-event catalog/outbox/broker, worker execution, consumer UI/navigation or deployment changes.
- Out of scope: full Task payloads in general notices/Mercure/subscriber records, new identity/permission policy,
  automatic business cancellation on disconnect, a separate notification endpoint or invented capability setting.

### Approved protocol, sourcing and projection direction

Extend TASK-00162's explicit subscriptions/listen composition with notifications.taskIds and the official
io.modelcontextprotocol/tasks 2026-07-28 extension. Validate finite identities/counts, malformed/duplicate/empty lists
and mixed filters under documented safe behavior. Acknowledge only accepted requested IDs first; never disclose hidden
Task existence through rejected IDs, counts, error details or technical metadata. Missing client Tasks declaration on
a Task-notification request returns -32021 with the required capability shape before opening Task delivery.
No separate Task-notification extension setting is defined; do not invent one or advertise unsupported composition.

Preserve standard io.modelcontextprotocol/subscriptionId metadata on acknowledgment, every notification and graceful
completion, with the originating JSON-RPC ID's exact type. Concurrent callers reusing IDs remain isolated. Support
Task-only and mixed streams without sending unrequested families; do not require Prompts or subscriber-inspection
composition. Notification params contain the flat DetailedTask plus permitted notification metadata, not the tasks/get
response's whole result/envelope or a newly invented resultType. No notifications/progress or notifications/message
are added for Tasks; HTTP stream closure is distinct from tasks/cancel.

Read durable current state through shared application Queries using bounded cross-process checks for accepted Task IDs.
Do not depend solely on in-process listeners, general retained-notice capture or presenter SQL. After acknowledgment,
provide an authorized current-state baseline, including Tasks already terminal, then follow changes. Qualify changes
between filter acceptance, baseline read and later observations. Intermediate states may be coalesced; no captured
transition history, missed-frame replay or every-update promise is introduced. Storage/source failure must not look
like healthy idle delivery. Document finite checking cadence/work and freshness, not guaranteed zero latency.

Reuse the complete authorized Task projection used by tasks/get, retaining original operation/result/error/input
semantics and current status-specific required fields. Completed Tool isError:true remains completed; failed carries
its safe JSON-RPC error; cancellation intent is not cancelled outcome. Emit all required outstanding input, not a
truncated map or fabricated redacted-success result. Public form descriptions and private retained validation/answers
remain distinct. Read and notification projection share contracts, not competing authorization or shape logic.

### Approved ordering and recovery direction

Supply a comparable Task-scoped state revision on both tasks/get reads and notifications through protocol-compatible
namespaced metadata. Bind it atomically to the returned state/projection; arrival order, clocks or lastUpdatedAt alone
cannot establish freshness. Use the lifecycle's coherent read/version mechanism without exposing a raw global sequence
or private mutation counter merely because one exists. Define revision comparison, permitted scope/context, metadata
collision handling, persistence across processes/reconnects, exhaustion and safe restart when comparison is unavailable.
Changes to readable state/retention metadata must not falsely share an equal revision while representing incompatible
state; duplicate/equal responses must have documented meaning. Concealed fields/changes must not leak through revision
gaps or metadata. Exact representation/privacy binding is implementation design within these accepted guarantees.

Qualify delayed get responses versus newer live frames, baseline races, equal timestamps, coalesced changes and reconnect
reads/new subscriptions. Document the consumer merge/comparison contract: a conforming caller can reject stale arrivals,
not merely trust network order. Common does not control clients that ignore the contract or implement browser UI.
If scope/visibility changes make revisions incomparable, require explicit safe restart rather than pretending newer.
Revision metadata is neither permission, transition history, notice-recovery position nor authoritative Workflow state.

A reconnect creates a new subscription, not a resumed saved stream. tasks/get retrieves currently authorized retained
state; it does not promise recovery of every missed notification. Expired/missing state remains unavailable without
inventing output, cancellation or an absence cause. Delivery failure/disconnect never rolls back state, renews retention,
replays external effects or submits business cancellation. Preserve independent polling/input/cancel composition.
Any read/projection/metadata adaptation required for this integration belongs here without a reverse TASK-00168 blocker.

### Approved disclosure, lifecycle and bounds direction

Reauthorize immediately before each full-state disclosure, including queued/baseline output and individual protected
state/input/result/error fields. Earlier tasks/get/opening access, generic feed/catalog permission, intended recipients,
capability declarations, human access or SUPER_ADMIN labels are not grants. Opening cannot freeze authorization.
If current permission, availability or budget prevents a complete safe notification, stop affected delivery honestly
rather than emit partial state/private diagnostics or silently claim continued coverage. Specify safe closure behavior
for Task-only/mixed streams and graceful versus abrupt failures under the existing lifecycle. Sent bytes cannot be retracted.

Reuse TASK-00162's owned registrations, requested/accepted filter tracking, heartbeats, freshness/closure, expiry and
bounded cleanup. Preserve taskIds only as permitted technical context; never persist Task bodies/private answers in
subscriber records. Technical metadata is independently protected and proves neither current permission nor receipt.
Do not add a duplicate tracking store or make operator inspection a prerequisite. Compatibility of generic optional-family
filter representation remains binding; no Task-specific inspection feature is required for this outcome.

Bound accepted IDs, active streams, Query work/cadence, pending latest snapshots, encoded frame bytes and lifetime.
Coalescing under load must preserve current-state semantics without unbounded queues or a false delivered-history claim.
Qualify full encoded output including nested results/text companions, escaping/multibyte data, revision/subscription/
central metadata, JSON-RPC envelope and SSE framing. Application-valid stored data may exceed the serving budget;
reject/stop safely without truncation or sending a partial oversized frame. McpResult::boundedComplete covers only a
result, not this final stream frame. Exact limits and safe error/completion budgeting remain implementation design.

Preserve guarded POST/Origin/mirrors/request bounds/admission/authentication and affected native response/emitter behavior.
Reuse actual live streaming rather than buffering or a second transport. No transaction spans stream/network waits;
technical writes must not commit/roll back unrelated caller work. Idle waits/keepalives, slow readers, abrupt termination,
source/tracking failures and shutdown follow documented SAPI/backpressure limits, not instant-disconnect guarantees.

Full-state notifications never enter the general retained notice store or indiscriminate Mercure output. No invocation
of those publishers is required for this live path. Consumers may separately publish safe references under their own
current decisions; a reference grants neither Task read access nor full-state disclosure. Lifecycle notification is not
an authoritative domain Event, Workflow transition, receipt or business success.
