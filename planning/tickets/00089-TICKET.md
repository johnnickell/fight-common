# Create the Canonical Fight Identity Asset Family

## Outcome

Turn the approved Inward Port — No Lower Rail direction into deterministic production assets that identify the
Fight family and Fight Common consistently in documentation, GitHub README, favicon, monochrome, reversed, light,
and dark contexts.

## Scope

- In scope: editable family mark, Fight Common lockup, full-color, one-color, reversed, theme, favicon,
  README-safe and social exports, a small-size specimen, and concise usage guidance.
- Out of scope: profile publication, lockups for unrequested Fight packages, animation, merchandise, a broad
  corporate brand system, or redesigning the approved geometry.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The canonical vector preserves the dark structural `F`, inbound steel approach rail, kiln-orange active
      port, one upper ownership rail, and open lower counterspace.
- [x] The `FIGHT` wordmark remains stable while the `COMMON` descriptor can be replaced for a future product.
- [x] Full-color, one-color, reversed, light-surface, dark-surface, favicon, README-safe, and social assets derive
      from the same deterministic geometry.
- [x] A specimen demonstrates recognizable, unclipped output at 64px, 32px, and 16px without effects.
- [x] Vector assets have valid intrinsic dimensions and view boxes and contain no unnecessary embedded raster,
      remote dependency, script, or inaccessible text substitute.
- [x] Minimum size, clear space, approved colors, background use, and prohibited distortions are documented
      concisely beside the assets.
- [x] Meaning survives monochrome output and never depends on gradients, shadows, or animation.
- [x] Applicable graphical boundaries meet the approved theme contrast requirements.

## Verification

- Validate vector structure and references with deterministic asset checks.
- Render and human-review the approved specimen in full color, one color, reversed, and at 64px, 32px, and 16px.
- Run `./bin/planning-check`, `git diff --check`, and the canonical `./bin/build`.
