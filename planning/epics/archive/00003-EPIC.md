# Thin Release Certification and Publication

## Destination

Certify an exact Fight Common candidate with real product, dependency, archive, manifest, and installed-consumer
evidence, then keep merge, tag, GitHub publication, and Packagist qualification as separately authorized human
effects.

## Decisions

- ADR 0025 replaces the simulated release framework with `./bin/release certify <version>`.
- Everyday PHPUnit and coverage protect only consumer-runtime `src/`; remaining release PHP stays in static and
  architecture checks.
- Certification is local and verification-only. It produces a compact record and archive for one clean full commit.
- Publication and Packagist observation use provider-native tools after separate authorization; they are not
  simulated by repository fakes or folded into certification.
- Completed TASK-00032 through TASK-00034 and TASK-00040 remain historical outcomes, but their machinery is superseded.
- TASK-00036 through TASK-00039 and TASK-00042 through TASK-00043 close `wontfix` rather than extending the retired framework.

## Scope

Thin certification, exact package and dependency evidence, separately authorized publication, and independently
verified Packagist installation.

## Out of Scope

Release state machines, effect ledgers, provider fakes, crash controls, automated maintenance and patch routing,
operator skill suites, hosted-CI certification, and bundled external effects.
